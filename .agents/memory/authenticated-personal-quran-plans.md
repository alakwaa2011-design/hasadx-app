---
name: Authenticated personal Quran plans
description: Separation rules for personal memorization in teacher and student readers on a shared device.
---

Personal Quran plans and self-assessments remain device-local and separate for anonymous reading and for each authenticated teacher or student. A student assigned ward may link to the personal plan, but the actual plan and practice must open in independent practice, never inside the assigned ward. An open personal memorization session must end if its confirmed account identity changes or verification fails.

**Why:** Shared devices and cached login identities could expose or alter another account's plan; carrying a ward's range or mode into independent practice blurs self-assessment with teacher-authorized progress.

**How to apply:** Confirm the current session before loading an account's local plan; do not trust a cached identity when fresh verification fails. Keep role and account boundaries in local persistence. Treat ward-to-practice navigation as a change of reader context, and never record personal assessments as assigned-ward submissions or verified mastery.

The guided “اربط” step in personal memorization links from the first ayah chosen in the plan through the current guided ayah, even if the span crosses a surah boundary. Resuming the step must keep that span rather than start from the current ayah alone.

**Why:** The learner expects linking to revisit the verses they started memorizing, not to repeat only the newest verse.

**How to apply:** Keep the guided assessment target separate from the linked playback start; never reset the latter to the current verse when advancing or resuming.
