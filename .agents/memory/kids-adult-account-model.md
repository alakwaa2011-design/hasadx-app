---
name: Kids adult account model
description: Defines who owns and launches individual and classroom play in Hasaad Kids.
---

Hasaad Kids does not need a separate parent account. The teacher account is also the adult/parent account and must offer both individual child play and classroom-board play.

**Why:** The product owner explicitly corrected the earlier assumption that parents and teachers need separate account flows. Requiring a second adult identity would add friction and contradict the intended experience.

**How to apply:** Adult settings, age/language selection, and activity launch live behind the teacher account. From there, the adult can hand the device to a child for individual play or launch the same activity on the classroom board. Do not introduce a separate parent login unless the product owner later changes this decision.
