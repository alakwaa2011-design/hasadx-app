---
name: Presentation control discoverability
description: Teacher-facing presentation controls must be nearby and remain visible during class.
---

Keep activity launch and reveal controls visibly associated with the activity they operate on, not separated by the full width of the screen. Put launch and previous/next controls inside the slide's joined frame or directly against its edges, not in detached panels or at distant viewport edges. Essential presentation controls should remain discoverable while the teacher is idle.

**Why:** The user reported that activity buttons were far from their titles and other controls did not appear during presenting. They clarified that merely grouping buttons in separate panels still leaves them too far away: controls should be inside the slide or at least directly attached, with clear colors. During a lesson, teachers should not have to search for the next action or move a pointer just to find it.

**How to apply:** Preserve this priority in future presentation UI changes. Attach controls to the actual printed slide bounds, not the viewport's letterbox; reserve their height so they never cover lesson content. Review wide projector layouts, portrait phones and short landscape screens, and ensure slide-navigation hit areas cannot intercept activity controls.
