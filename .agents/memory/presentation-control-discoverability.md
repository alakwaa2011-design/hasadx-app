---
name: Presentation control discoverability
description: Teacher-facing presentation controls must be nearby and remain visible during class.
---

Keep activity launch and reveal controls visibly associated with the activity they operate on, not separated by the full width of the screen. Essential presentation controls should remain discoverable while the teacher is idle.

**Why:** The user reported that activity buttons were far from their titles and other controls did not appear during presenting. During a lesson, teachers should not have to search for the next action or move a pointer just to find it.

**How to apply:** Preserve this priority in future presentation UI changes. Review wide projector layouts as well as narrow screens, and ensure slide-navigation hit areas cannot intercept activity controls.
