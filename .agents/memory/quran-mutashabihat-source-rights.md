description: Licensing boundary for expanding the Quran verbal-similarities index.
---

Bundle Quran similarity relations only when their **dataset-specific** reuse terms permit redistribution. Keep reviewed, clearly licensed relations separate from unreviewed or differently licensed records.

**Why:** QUL publicly documents phrase-to-ayah mappings, but its download required sign-in when checked on 2026-09-26. Its FAQ says licensing varies by resource. The MIT license on its software repository is not proof that the separately distributed dataset may be bundled. Another public curated dataset explicitly licenses its reviewed pairs CC BY 4.0 while stating its pending pairs have no formal license.

**How to apply:** Before expanding the index with QUL or another source, obtain an authorized export and check the specific resource's rights; do not scrape preview pages or infer dataset rights from a repository's code license. Preserve attribution and distinguish verified shared wording from broader editorial similarity categories.
