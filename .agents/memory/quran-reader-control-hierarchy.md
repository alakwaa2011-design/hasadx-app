---
name: Quran reader control hierarchy
description: UX rationale for prioritizing the Quran page over toolbar actions and consolidating repeat controls
---

Keep the Quran text visually primary. Expose navigation, search, recitation, and guided memorization directly; treat the personal plan as a path into memorization, not a competing primary action. Ordinary listening repetition should have one entry that offers ayah or segment mode; guided memorization's stage-specific practice count remains contextual to that lesson.

**Why:** The reader found many equal-weight header tools and separate ayah/segment repeat settings distracting. The plan is useful to some readers, but not a universal top-level need. A repeat control belongs where listeners can reach it during playback rather than hidden among reciter choices.

**How to apply:** When adding Quran tools, assign them to reading navigation, audio playback, guided learning, or secondary reading preferences before adding a header button. Do not duplicate repetition configuration in multiple audio panels.
