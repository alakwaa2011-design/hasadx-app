---
name: Quran reveal questions
description: Interaction and compatibility rules for concise verse-recall questions in Quran competitions.
---

Concise verse-recall questions use a think-then-reveal interaction followed by explicit self-assessment: full, partial, or unknown. Revealing an answer alone must never record a wrong answer.

Multi-point recall questions use two to five independently referenced answer points. After reveal, the participant reports how many points they recalled; award 10 points per recalled item, one star for a partial harvest, and three stars only for recalling every item.

**Why:** These prompts ask the participant to recall a verse and often cannot be represented honestly by four plausible choices. Treating reveal as failure also penalizes learning behavior.

**How to apply:** Keep these question types available in individual quiz play and admin import/edit flows. Exclude them from challenge and tournament modes until those modes gain an explicit reveal-and-judge interaction.
