---
name: Reward avatar visual identity
description: Official visual direction and interaction expectations for avatars in Hasaad's reward system.
---

Use polished illustrated adventure characters as the official avatar language throughout the reward system. The selectable cast should include varied ages, children, modestly dressed characters, and friendly animal adventure companions. Do not expose emoji or legacy avatars as fallback choices in reward avatar selection.

Characters should feel alive in the reward journey: after a successful point grant, prominently feature the awarded student's character in a short celebratory motion rather than leaving it as a static thumbnail.

When a student account is linked to a teacher-managed student profile, every student-facing surface must prefer the linked profile's avatar. The older account avatar remains only a compatibility fallback when the linked profile has no avatar.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved the illustrated character direction as Hasaad's official reward-system identity and rejected the older emoji options because they reduce quality and break visual consistency. Linked profiles can be updated after account creation, so preferring the account copy makes the same student appear as two different characters.

**How to apply:** Preserve the established illustrated style when adding reward avatars or visual feedback. Keep celebrations tied to confirmed successful grants, respect reduced-motion preferences, and do not change point or persistence logic for visual enhancements. Apply linked-profile-first precedence to dashboards, public profiles, and game/session identity responses.
