---
name: Third-party recitation provenance
description: Copyright due diligence for externally sourced Qur'an audio used in shareable media
---

An audio file listed as CC0 or Creative Commons on Wikimedia Commons is not automatically safe to redistribute if the Commons uploader sourced a commercial reciter's recording from a different site and did not document the right to relicense it.

**Why:** A recitation suitable for a classroom video was marked CC0 on Commons but named an external Qur'an-audio site as its original source. The displayed license is a claim about the file, not independent proof of the uploader's authority. By contrast, a reciter-uploaded recording described as their own work has clearer provenance.

**How to apply:** For public video releases, verify the original recording owner's explicit permission or replace uncertain audio with directly licensed/owned human recitation. Credit the performance and disclose any remaining provenance uncertainty instead of presenting a Commons label as definitive authorization.
