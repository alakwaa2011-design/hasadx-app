Tug of War has two primary launch choices that must always be visible as peers: “على السبورة” and “على أجهزة الطلاب”. Do not label device mode as “start as teacher,” and do not hide board mode in advanced launch options. Link copying is a secondary sharing action.

**Why:** The teacher could not find device mode and found the previous host terminology ambiguous. The narrow settings flow also felt inconsistent with Wameeth on desktop.

**How to apply:** Use Wameeth's wide responsive page container for question selection, settings, and the following waiting/play screen. Keep the two launch modes explicit and preserve their distinct existing handlers.
