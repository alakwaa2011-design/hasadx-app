name: X O remote team participation
description: Approved participation model and the distinction between joining a match and launching a new one.
---

In X O remote mode, students join one shared match and are assigned to balanced teams. A different connected student represents each team on its next turn; only that representative answers and places the earned mark. If the representative disconnects, another connected teammate takes over without receiving a new time allowance.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose “إصلاح الأعطال + تناوب الطلاب” rather than allowing every teammate to answer simultaneously. Keep the local classroom/board mode unchanged; its participation rules are intentionally different.

**How to apply:** Keep current-room student sharing separate from permanent activity sharing. Copy and QR inside a match must join that match. Permanent activity links continue creating independent new matches and should be labelled accordingly, not globally reinterpreted as live-room links.

In remote mode, hide the permanent new-match link from setup. The teacher must be able to copy the actual room's student join link before entering the game, as in Wameeth.

**Why:** The user explicitly clarified that removing the permanent-link feature must not force the teacher to enter the room just to copy its link.

**How to apply:** Make room-link copying available from setup/preparation, separate from the teacher's action to enter the game. The copied link must join that room, not launch independent matches.

Treat a distributed room link as belonging to one room. Do not silently replace its room after a connection failure or a settings edit.

**Why:** Students can join before the teacher enters. Replacing the room while reusing the setup screen would leave those students waiting in a different match. The user requested explicit edits to the existing room while it is waiting, with PIN, link and student identities unchanged.

**How to apply:** Reuse the prepared room for repeat copies, reloads, and teacher entry. Save changes only through an authorized waiting-room update, serialized against match start. Room edits are not edits to the permanent saved activity.
