- Adaptation date and source dataset status: 2026-09-26; 283 entries marked `curated`.

This adaptation retains only each curated pair's canonical verse references and
category. It omits source notes and all other descriptive text. Entries marked
`pending` or `dropped` are excluded entirely because the source identifies no
usable license for those entries.

The additional repeated-verse and shared-phrase matches shown in the app are
calculated from the project's existing Q-Complex Quran text, not copied from
this relations dataset. Comparison ignores an opening surah basmala when it
is prefixed to an ayah, includes matching complete short ayahs, and otherwise
requires at least three consecutive matching words. This is an exact-text
search, not a claim to catalog every editorial or near-wording similarity.
