Build a premium Arabic RTL landing page for an educational platform called "حصاد", but position it clearly as a platform for competitions and activities, not just general education.

Design direction:
- Use the THIRD design concept as the main visual foundation.
- Use the SECOND design concept only to soften the experience with better Arabic typography, educational warmth, and a friendlier tone.
- The result should feel modern, bold, premium, and memorable, while still clearly educational and Arabic.

Brand positioning:
- Hisad is primarily a competitions and activities platform.
- The homepage must communicate this immediately.
- The messaging should focus on creating competitions, publishing activities, student participation by code, and tracking results.

Visual style:
- Arabic only
- RTL layout
- Premium dark or semi-dark hero section
- Deep charcoal or dark green background tones
- Green glow or subtle lighting inspired by modern SaaS/product landing pages
- White or lightly tinted cards for contrast in sections below
- Elegant Arabic typography
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Modern shadows, gradients, and rounded corners
- Avoid making it look like a finance dashboard or crypto product
- Avoid making it too playful or childish

Main message direction:
Use a headline such as:
"منصة عربية لصناعة المسابقات والأنشطة التعليمية"
or
"أنشئ مسابقات تفاعلية وأنشطة تعليمية في دقائق"

Supporting text should explain:
- teachers can create competitions and activities easily
- students can join instantly with a code
- results and submissions can be tracked in one place

Page structure:
1. Navbar
   - Arabic logo on the right
   - minimal menu
   - login button
   - clean and premium look

2. Hero section
   - Strong bold Arabic headline focused on competitions and activities
   - Short supporting paragraph
   - Primary CTA: "أنشئ مسابقتك الآن"
   - Secondary CTA: "استعرض المسابقات الجاهزة"
   - Optional student CTA: "أدخل كود المسابقة"
   - Large premium visual area inspired by the third design
   - Use subtle green glow, layered depth, and polished visual composition

3. Quick participation strip or join card
   - Make joining by code very obvious
   - This is important because the platform includes student participation in competitions
   - Include labeled input, not placeholder only
   - Button text: "انضم الآن"

4. Competitions and activities highlights
   - Create a section showing the platform’s core strength:
     - interactive competitions
     - educational activities
     - quick participation
     - organized submissions
     - teacher-friendly management
   - Use premium cards with concise Arabic copy

5. Stats section
   - Show platform traction with elegant cards
   - Example Arabic stats:
     - +61 نشاطًا تعليميًا
     - +26 معلّمًا مسجلًا
     - +67 تسليمًا مكتملًا
     - +737 طالبًا مشاركًا

6. How it works section
   - Simple 3-step explanation for teachers and students
   - Example:
     1. أنشئ المسابقة أو النشاط
     2. شارك الرابط أو الكود مع الطلاب
     3. تابع المشاركات والنتائج بسهولة

7. Final CTA section
   - Strong close focused on action
   - Example:
     "ابدأ أول مسابقة لك اليوم"

Typography and Arabic feel:
- Use a strong Arabic font style or font stack that feels elegant and modern
- Improve Arabic spacing and heading rhythm
- Keep the Arabic wording clear, direct, and professional
- Do not use weak generic educational copy

Experience goals:
- The first screen should instantly communicate that this is a modern Arabic competitions platform
- It should feel more distinctive than a standard education website
- It should balance premium modern design with educational clarity
- It should be optimized for conversion and trust

Technical requirements:
- Build a production-quality landing page
- Use responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Use reusable components
- Clean code structure
- HTML/CSS/JS or React is acceptable

Final objective:
Create a landing page that is visually based mostly on the third concept, emotionally softened by the second concept, and strategically focused on Hisad as a competitions and activities platform.
