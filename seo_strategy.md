# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing and informational pages: `/`, `/about`, `/faq`, `/privacy`, `/terms`
- Public discovery and community pages: `/games`, `/public/games`, `/install`, `/feedback`, `/leaderboard`
- Public shareable entity pages: `/t/:idOrSlug`, `/u/:idOrSlug`, `/p/:id`
- Public Islamic quiz surfaces under `/islamic/**` when reachable without teacher/organizer authentication

## Out of scope
- Authenticated teacher routes under `/teacher/**`
- Authenticated organizer routes under `/organizer/**`
- Student-only authenticated routes and classroom/game-play surfaces that are not intended as landing pages
- Internal API routes under `/api/**`

## Target audience
- Arabic-speaking teachers, schools, and education organizers looking for interactive teaching, quizzes, assignments, and AI-assisted classroom tools.

## Primary keywords
- Unknown — likely centered on Arabic interactive learning, educational quizzes, interactive presentations, assignments, and AI-powered teaching tools. Refine when keyword research is available.

## Indexation notes
- Authentication and account utility pages such as `/login` and `/register` are public but low-value for search discovery and should generally remain `noindex`.
